## ⚡ 2. Our 3 Signature Differentiating Features

### 1. Intelligent Trade-Specific Emergency Triage
Unlike generic virtual assistants or human operators who read basic scripts, JustMascot's Voice AI is trained specifically on trade workflows (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Roofing).

* **How It Works**: During an inbound call, the AI analyzes keywords, distress signals, and caller intent in real time.
* **Emergency Recognition**: Differentiates between a routine annual tune-up quote and a Tier-1 trade emergency (e.g. *active water burst in basement, main sewer line backup, gas odor, or 100°F heat wave AC outage*).
* **Instant Prioritization**: Immediately tags the job as high-priority emergency dispatch and routes emergency telemetry to on-call supervisors.

---

### 2. Autonomous 120-Minute Emergency Schedule Shifting
Traditional FSM software requires a human dispatcher to manually phone 5 different customers when an emergency call comes in. **JustMascot handles this autonomously.**

* **How It Works**: When a high-value emergency job ($1,500+) comes in and all master technicians are currently booked on routine non-urgent maintenance, the dispatch engine evaluates route schedules.
* **Autonomous Buffer Shifting**: The AI can automatically push back a routine 1:00 PM filter replacement job by up to **120 minutes** (to 3:00 PM).
* **Automated Client Courtesy Notification**: The AI triggers an automated SMS to the non-urgent customer:
  > *"Hi [Customer Name], your technician is responding to a neighborhood safety emergency. Your arrival window has been updated to 3:00 PM. Reply 1 to confirm or 2 to reschedule."*
* **Business Impact**: Opens an immediate 2-hour technician window to capture the $1,500 emergency job without losing the routine appointment.

---

### 3. 24/7 After-Hours Revenue Recovery Engine
70% of homeowners who experience an after-hours plumbing or HVAC emergency will hang up and call the next contractor on Google if their call goes to voicemail.

* **Acts Like a Top 1% Dispatcher**: The AI speaks naturally, verifies customer location, confirms trade category, checks calendar availability, and locks in the appointment slot before the caller hangs up.
* **Zero Phone Tag**: Eliminates the "morning call backlog" where office staff spend 2 hours every Monday returning missed weekend voicemails only to find callers already hired someone else.
* **Massive Payroll Savings**: Delivers 24/7 dispatch capabilities for less than 15% of the cost of hiring a full-time weekend/night office staff member.

## 🥊 3. Competitive Battlecards for Sales & Marketing

### Battlecard A: Facing "We already use ServiceTitan / Jobber"
* **Prospect Objection**: *"We already have ServiceTitan/Jobber for our dispatch board and invoices."*
* **Our Response / Position**: 
  > *"ServiceTitan and Jobber are fantastic databases for tracking jobs once they are booked, but they don’t answer your phone when a customer calls at 8 PM on a Sunday. JustMascot doesn't replace your CRM—it acts as your 24/7 AI Voice Receptionist that feeds confirmed bookings and emergency jobs directly into your dispatch workflow so you never lose leads to voicemail."*

---

### Battlecard B: Facing "We already use an Answering Service"
* **Prospect Objection**: *"We pay an answering service $500/month to take messages after hours."*
* **Our Response / Position**: 
  > *"Answering services just take paper messages and leave you playing phone tag the next morning. By then, the customer has already called your competitor. JustMascot actually books the appointment directly into your schedule in under 60 seconds, triages true trade emergencies, and doesn't charge you $4 per minute just to hold on line."*
